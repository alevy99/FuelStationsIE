Fuel Watch Ireland

Community-powered fuel availability

About the App

Fuel Watch Ireland is a community-driven application that allows users to report and view real-time information about fuel stations across Ireland.

With this app you can check the current availability of petrol and diesel at nearby stations, view the latest community-reported fuel prices, and stay informed about any purchase limits — whether by spend cap (€) or litre cap (L).

All data is provided by users like you. The more people report, the more accurate and up-to-date the information becomes.

How It Works

🔍
Find stations

Browse fuel stations by county or use your GPS location to find stations near you.

📊
View prices & availability

See the latest community-reported prices, stock availability, and purchase limits.

📝
Report updates

Tap + Update on any station to report current prices, availability, or limits.
